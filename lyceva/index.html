The platform

One platform. Fewer disconnected tools.

Schools often rely on spreadsheets, paper records, messaging apps, payment tools, and separate administrative systems that do not work together.

Lyceva is being built to bring those workflows into a single secure platform.

  1. ADM

    Admissions & Enrollment

    Manage applications, student onboarding, enrollment records, and admissions workflows from one place.

  2. STU

    Student Management

    Maintain structured student profiles, academic records, class information, and important school data.

  3. ACA

    Academics

    Support classes, subjects, assessments, results, attendance, and academic administration.

  4. FEE

    Fees & Payments

    Track school fees, payment records, balances, invoices, and financial activity.

  5. STF

    Staff Administration

    Manage staff records, roles, permissions, and internal operational workflows.

  6. PAR

    Parent Engagement

    Give schools a structured way to communicate important information with parents and guardians.

  7. RPT

    Reports & Insights

    Turn school data into useful reports and operational insights for administrators.

AI

AI built into school operations

Lyceva is being designed with AI as part of the platform, not as a separate chatbot.

Using technologies such as Claude, Lyceva aims to help authorized school staff:

  • Summarize operational and student information
  • Generate administrative reports
  • Draft school communications
  • Assist with admissions workflows
  • Reduce repetitive administrative work
  • Ask questions about permitted school data using natural language
  • Surface useful operational insights

AI functionality will be designed around role-based permissions and school data boundaries.

Architecture

Built for secure multi-school operations

Lyceva is being developed as a multi-tenant SaaS platform, allowing each school to operate within its own protected workspace.

Each institution will have its own users, roles, permissions, records, workflows, and administrative environment.
